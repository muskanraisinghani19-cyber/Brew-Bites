Welcome to Brew & Bites

Brew & Bites started with a simple idea: to bring people together over good food and great coffee. We believe that a café should be a place where everyone feels comfortable and welcome.

Whether you are looking for a quick coffee before college, a snack during your break or a peaceful place to meet your friends, Brew & Bites is the perfect corner for you.

Our menu includes freshly prepared beverages, tasty snacks and delicious desserts. Every item is selected to make your café experience enjoyable.
